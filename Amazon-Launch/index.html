EverydayPaks Amazon Launch Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon & Walmart Growth

Strategy Overview

EverydayPaks is entering Amazon with a strong opportunity to stand out in the GLP-1 support category through a product format that is simple, transparent, and built for everyday use.

The initial focus is to successfully launch the GLP-1 Support Pak, establish trust and visibility, and build a strong foundation for repeat purchase and Subscribe & Save.

Launch Priorities
  • Own the Daily Pak format as the key point of difference.
  • Build trust and social proof for a new brand with no existing Amazon history.
  • Launch with strong SEO, clear product education, and conversion-focused creative.
  • Use pricing strategically to support the launch while the brand builds trust.
  • Build toward Subscribe & Save and long-term retention, not just one-time orders.
  • Use the first 90 days to learn, optimize, and prepare for the next stage of growth.
Core Brand Message:
One Pak. One Time. Everyday.
Pre-Launch Foundation

Complete the key account, compliance, and inventory requirements before launch to avoid preventable delays.

Brand Registry
  • Apply for Amazon Brand Registry using the pending EP EVERYDAYPAKS trademark.
  • Prepare clear, real-life product photos showing the EverydayPaks branding for Brand Registry verification.
Supplement Compliance

Prepare for Amazon dietary supplement verification ahead of launch:

  • Current accredited third-party cGMP certification.
  • Existing COAs and product testing reports.
  • Final product label and clear real-life product photos.
  • If Amazon triggers verification, initiate it directly through Seller Central and complete validation/testing through an Amazon-authorized TIC provider, such as Eurofins.
  • Account for potential testing costs and lead time in the launch timeline.
Growth Area 1: Own the Daily Pak Format

The Daily Pak format should be one of the biggest points of difference for EverydayPaks.

Instead of buying, storing, sorting, and remembering multiple supplement bottles, the customer gets their daily routine together in one pre-portioned Pak.

Key Recommendations
Make simplicity the hero.

Build the positioning around:

One Pak→One Daily Routine→Less Sorting→Less Clutter→Less to Remember

Lean into the brand's existing language:

  • One Pak. One Time. Everyday.
  • Simple on purpose.
  • One less thing to think about.
  • Grab it. Take it. Get on with your day.
  • Less clutter. Less sorting. Less overthinking.
Make travel part of the story.

Going away for two days? Take two Paks.

Show how the format removes the need to travel with multiple supplement bottles and makes it easier to stay consistent away from home.

Own the format visually.

The Daily Pak gives us an opportunity to look different in search results.

Explore a main image that makes the individual Pak and product format immediately recognizable rather than blending into a category dominated by traditional supplement packaging.

Carry the Daily Pak visual identity through the image stack, Premium A+, Store, video, and advertising.

Growth Area 2: Build Trust & Social Proof

EverydayPaks will launch as a completely new brand with no Amazon sales history, reviews, or existing customer recognition.

Building trust quickly will be critical. We need to give customers enough confidence in both the product itself and the brand behind it to try something new.

The goal is to build trust from multiple directions:

Product Transparency & Trust Signals→Early Social Proof→Creator Content & UGC→Product Insert→Ongoing Review Generation
Key Recommendations
Build trust directly into the listing.

The listing itself needs to do as much of the trust-building work as possible from Day 1.

Use the Daily Pak format to clearly show exactly what customers are taking and what each component is there to support:

Foundation Blend— Multivitamin + mineral support.
Metabolic Balance— Metabolic wellness support.
Recharge— B12 support.
Gut Balance— Probiotic support.

Use the image stack, Premium A+, video, and Store to visually open the Pak and explain the components.

Pair this transparency with every substantiated quality and trust signal available. Current packaging already includes:

Non-GMOGluten-FreeNo Added HormonescGMP Compliant

The goal is for customers to quickly understand what they're taking, why it's there, and why they can feel confident in the product.

Leverage the Existing Company Network for Early Social Proof

Use the company's existing network to build initial product awareness, encourage early purchases, and help establish credibility during launch.

  • Ensure all purchases and review-related activities comply with Amazon's policies.
  • Guidelines will be provided on how to approach this compliantly and effectively.
Expand social proof through Creator Connections and UGC.

Once the listing has established initial traction and customer reviews, leverage Creator Connections and user-generated content (UGC) to build credibility and increase product visibility.

  • Activate Creator Connections to encourage creators to showcase, demonstrate, and talk about the product.
  • Use authentic creator content to reinforce product benefits, transparency, and customer trust.
Use the product insert to support the post-purchase experience.

Use the planned product insert to continue the brand experience after purchase and include an Amazon-compliant review request to support review growth as order volume builds.

The goal is to build enough trust around the product from Day 1 that the launch does not depend on having a large number of Amazon reviews to convert.

Growth Area 3: Build Search Visibility & a Strong Listing

The launch listing needs to do two things well from Day 1:

Get found + convert the traffic we generate.

EverydayPaks should launch with a fully optimized, conversion-ready PDP. We do not want to drive launch traffic to an incomplete listing and add core content later.

Key Recommendations
Complete full Amazon SEO research.

Build the listing around:

  • Highest-value GLP-1 support searches.
  • Relevant nutritional support searches.
  • Ingredient searches.
  • High-intent long-tail opportunities.
  • Competitor keyword gaps.
  • Relevant customer needs/search behavior where compliant.

Apply SEO across the full listing:

Title→Bullets→Attributes→Backend Search Terms→Images→Premium A+→Advertising
Make the product easy for Amazon to understand.

Fully complete relevant attributes and structure the content so Amazon can clearly understand:

  • What the product is.
  • Who it is for.
  • What's inside each Pak.
  • What each component supports.
  • How it is used.
  • What makes the format different.

This should support both traditional Amazon search and Amazon's AI-assisted shopping experiences.

Treat the SERP as one package.

Optimize:

Main Image+Title+Price/Offer+Reviews

The Daily Pak format should be a major part of the visual strategy and help EverydayPaks stand out before the shopper even reaches the PDP.

Launch — Fully Optimized PDP

Launch with:

  • Fully optimized front-end copy and SEO.
  • Complete backend search terms and relevant attributes.
  • Full conversion-focused image stack.
  • Premium A+ Content.
  • Brand Story.
  • Mobile-optimized content across the PDP.

The entire PDP should be built as one connected conversion experience, with consistent positioning, messaging, SEO, product education, and trust signals throughout.

Phase 2 — Expand the Brand Experience

Once the core PDP is live:

  • Build the Amazon Storefront.
  • Add educational/product video.
  • Expand UGC and creator content as assets become available.
  • Continue optimizing the PDP based on actual search, CTR, conversion, and customer behavior.
Growth Area 4: Use Pricing to Support the Launch

EverydayPaks will launch as a new brand without established Amazon reviews or customer recognition.

Starting at $19.99 makes it easier for customers to give a new product and brand a try while we build initial sales, reviews, and conversion history.

From there, we will gradually test higher price points as the product builds stronger ratings, more reviews, and stable conversion.

Pricing Roadmap
LAUNCH & LEARN — $19.99
Build initial sales, reviews, rating, and conversion history.
FIRST PRICE TEST — $21.99
Test the first increase once the product has built stronger social proof and conversion supports moving the price up.
CONTINUE TESTING — $22.99
Continue increasing gradually while monitoring the impact on rating/reviews, conversion, sales, and advertising performance.
IDEAL EVERYDAY PRICE — $24.99
Work toward $24.99 if the product has built enough trust and Amazon performance supports it.
How We'll Make Pricing Decisions

Price increases will not be tied to a fixed timeline.

Before moving to the next price point, we will look at:

  • Rating & Reviews — Has the product built enough social proof to support a higher price?
  • Conversion — Is the listing converting strongly and consistently?
  • Sales — Are we maintaining healthy sales as the brand establishes itself?
  • Advertising Performance — Can we continue acquiring customers efficiently at the new price?
  • Margin — Does the change improve the overall economics without hurting growth?
  • Subscribe & Save — Are we successfully turning new customers into subscribers?

$24.99 is the ideal everyday price we want to work toward, but actual Amazon performance will determine where the final price lands and how quickly we get there.

This pricing approach supports the broader launch strategy: build trust first, then ask the customer to pay more as that trust and performance strengthen.

Growth Area 5: Build the Subscriber Base

This is a daily-use product. Long-term success should therefore be measured not only by how many customers we acquire, but by how many customers stay with the product.

Key Recommendations
Build Subscribe & Save into the strategy from the beginning.

Once eligible:

  • Enroll in Subscribe & Save.
  • Start with a 10% discount.
  • Add a first-order Subscribe & Save coupon when eligible to support subscriber acquisition.
Support subscription through the listing.

Build messaging around:

Daily Product→Daily Habit→Easy Replenishment

The goal is to make Subscribe & Save feel like the natural way to buy the product.

Learn the customer lifecycle.

We don't yet know what the real customer lifecycle looks like.

Use repeat behavior, subscriber retention, reviews, and customer feedback to understand how customers experience the product over time.

As that intelligence builds, expand the content to communicate the value of consistent use.

Potential future visual:

1 Month→3 Months→6 Months
Long-Term Goal:
Don't just sell the product. Build the habit.
Growth Area 6: Expand Reach Through Creator Connections

Creator Connections should be part of the growth strategy, but not from Day 1.

First establish some social proof and a healthy listing so creators are evaluating a product that already has customer validation.

Rollout
Launch→Initial Sales & Social Proof→Healthy Rating/CVR→Creator Connections→Scale
Key Recommendations

Once the listing has established an initial foundation:

  • Launch Creator Connections.
  • Monitor creator participation and attributed performance.
  • Identify the strongest creator angles and scale what works.
  • Build a growing library of creator content and UGC that can further support product visibility and trust.

The Daily Pak format gives creators plenty to work with:

  • What's inside my Pak.
  • Morning routine.
  • Opening and explaining the components.
  • No bottle sorting.
  • Work/on-the-go use.
  • Weekend/travel routine.
  • Everyday consistency.

Creator content should reinforce the same core positioning as the listing rather than creating a separate product story.

Creator Connections should support both reach and trust - getting the product in front of new customers while building more social proof around how EverydayPaks fits into real everyday routines.

Growth Area 7: Use Video to Educate & Convert

Use video to quickly show customers what EverydayPaks is, what's inside each Pak, and how easily it fits into an everyday routine.

Prioritize:

  • What's inside the Daily Pak and what each component supports.
  • How to use it.
  • Everyday routine and convenience.
  • Travel/on-the-go use.
  • Product, formula, and quality education.

Use video across the PDP, Premium A+, Store, and advertising where relevant.

Advertising Strategy
Goal

Get the GLP-1 Support Pak its first sales and reviews, then build rank for "glp 1 support supplement" and related terms, while keeping launch spend capped.

Where we are starting
  • No ASIN, reviews or sales history yet. The seller account is still being set up.
  • Good market to enter: about 325K monthly searches across 154 relevant keywords
  • We add campaigns in steps as reviews grow and scale only when the data supports it.
Campaigns we will build

Creative Strategy

Creative should support the same core story throughout Amazon:

Daily Pak→Simplicity→Transparency→Trust→Everyday Routine
Priority areas:
  • Make the Daily Pak visually distinctive
    • We can do this through developing a solid main image that stands out from competitors on SERP - showing the paks and exactly what’s inside will help separate us from competitors
    • Inclusion of a badge (e.g.competitor Etta Vita uses “made in the USA” badge) will also help build trust
  • Image stack should clearly emphasize key USPs within first 3 images (where most consumers convert)
  • Tile to explain the role of each component
  • Demonstrate ease of use through generated lifestyle imagery - allowing the consumer to envision themselves incorporating the product into their daily lives
  • Show travel/portability through imagery
  • Surface quality/trust signals
    • Good UGC can be included in the A+ content for social proof
  • Build mobile-first as this is where most consumers shop - we will also develop separate desktop imagery to ensure consumers have a quality experience on both formats
  • Support video and educational content
    • Video leads to much higher conversion - UGC is beneficial but branded educational content is incredibly valuable to further educate the consumer on ‘why THIS product’ and any more scientific-led information
  • Prepare adaptable New Year creative to target the ‘New Year, New You’ audience
  • Ensure the consumer journey is a seamless, simple to digest experience - from the image stack, to A+ content, brand story and storefront. This should easily guide consumers who require further information before making their purchase decision
  • Utilize cross-selling elements when additional products are available
  • Once subscribe and save is available, we will incorporate this in the imagery across all elements to encourage subscriptions and repeat purchase
